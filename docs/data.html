Data & measurement

Data digitization, harmonization, and linkage.

Research datasets

Italian Industrial Technology Adoption, 1911

I am digitizing province-by-industry tables from Italy’s 1911 industrial census, recording the number and characteristics of industrial motors. The construction harmonizes geographic and industry identifiers, removes aggregate rows, and reconciles detailed entries with published province totals.

Linked Inventor and Career Data

We are constructing a longitudinal dataset linking patent records to inventors’ education and employment histories. The data combine patenting activity with career spells, employer identifiers, and workplace locations, allowing individuals to be followed across firms and places over time.

U.S. Restaurant Metadata from Google Local, 2021

We are compiling restaurant business metadata from the McAuley Lab’s Google Local 2021 dataset. The construction combines U.S. state files, filters businesses by restaurant categories, and retains business identifiers, names, addresses, coordinates, ratings, and fields used to link establishments across data sources.
