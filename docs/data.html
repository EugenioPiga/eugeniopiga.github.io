Historical technology adoption

Italian Industrial Technology Adoption, 1911

Digitized province-by-industry information on industrial motors from Italy’s 1911 industrial census. The current work harmonizes industry and geographic identifiers and checks detailed entries against published totals.

Linked Inventor and Career Data

Business metadata

U.S. Restaurant Metadata from Google Local, 2021

A restaurant-focused compilation of the McAuley Lab’s Google Local business metadata. The work combines state files and retains business identifiers, locations, categories, ratings, and fields useful for establishment linkage.
