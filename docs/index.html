Eugenio Piga

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the University of California, San Diego.

I study technological change, innovation, and their effects on inequality. My research examines how new technologies affect firms and people, why innovation concentrates in particular places, and how these forces shape the distribution of income and opportunity.

I combine economic theory with experiments and large-scale data on workers, firms, and inventors.
